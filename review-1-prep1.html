Grammar Review — مراجعة القواعد

1. Present Simple

الاستخدام: للتعبير عن العادات والحقائق اليومية.

Subject + Verb (base) + Object
He/She/It + Verb + s
I check my phone every morning.
أنا أتفقد هاتفي كل صباح.
She reads ebooks before bedtime.
هي تقرأ كتباً إلكترونية قبل النوم.
They don't play online games.
هم لا يلعبون ألعاباً عبر الإنترنت.

2. Adverbs of Frequency

الاستخدام: لوصف معدل تكرار الفعل.

always (100%) → usually (90%) → often (70%) → sometimes (50%) → rarely (10%) → never (0%)

Subject + Adverb + Verb
⚠️ يأتي بعد verb to be: He is always busy.
I always check my phone.
أنا دائماً أتفقد هاتفي.
She never eats without looking at screens.
هي أبداً لا تأكل بدون النظر إلى الشاشات.

3. Present Continuous

الاستخدام: للتعبير عن حدث يحدث الآن.

Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing
🔑 كلمات دالة: now, at the moment, right now, currently
I am learning English now.
أتعلم الإنجليزية الآن.
She is studying for her test.
تدرس لاختبارها.

4. Can for Ability

الاستخدام: للتعبير عن القدرة.

Subject + can + Verb (base) + Object
Negative: Subject + can't + Verb (base)
I can speak English well.
أستطيع التحدث بالإنجليزية جيداً.
She can't swim very fast.
لا تستطيع السباحة بسرعة.

5. Past Simple

الاستخدام: للتعبير عن حدث بدأ وانتهى في الماضي.

Regular: Verb + ed
Irregular: go → went, have → had, be → was/were
Negative: didn't + Verb (base)
She worked hard in research.
عملت بجد في البحث.
She was born in Egypt in 1917.
وُلدت في مصر عام 1917.
He didn't go to school yesterday.
لم يذهب إلى المدرسة أمس.

Reading Comprehension

Passage 1: My Digital Day

Hi everyone! My name is Tarek, and I want to share my digital day with you. I always start my morning by checking my phone. I usually read three or four messages from my school friends. At breakfast, I often use my tablet to search for news. My favorite website gives me interesting facts about animals and space. I never eat without looking at my screen — I know it's not a good habit!

At school, we sometimes use the smartboard in our science class. After school, I always do my homework first. In the evening, I sometimes play online games with my friends. Before bedtime, I often read ebooks on my tablet. But I always use the "night mode" so the screen doesn't hurt my eyes.
1. What does Tarek do first in the morning?
He checks his phone
He eats breakfast
He plays games
2. Why does Tarek use night mode?
To save battery
To read faster
To protect his eyes

Passage 2: Dr. Samira Moussa

Dr. Samira Moussa was a true hero who continues to inspire people today. She was born in Egypt in 1917 and showed a passionate love for science from a young age. She was determined to study physics and became one of the first Egyptian women to get a Ph.D. in atomic radiation.

Samira was kind and always wanted to help her community. She believed that science should be used for peace. Her achievement in science and her courageous spirit made her a symbol of hope for many Egyptian girls.
3. When was Dr. Samira Moussa born?
In 1907
In 1917
In 1927
4. What was Dr. Samira passionate about?
Science
Art
Music

From: me@email.com
Subject: My Digital Day

Hi [Name],

How are you? I want to tell you about my digital day. When I get up, I always check my phone. I read messages from my friends and reply to them.

Then, I have breakfast while I use my tablet to search for the latest news. At school, teachers usually use a smartboard to help us learn well.

What about you? Can you write back and tell me about your digital day?

Yours,
[Your Name]

2. My Learning Plan (Unit 2)

My name is [Name], and I am a [visual/auditory/kinesthetic] learner. I learn best by [seeing/hearing/moving].

I like to use [colorful notes/mind maps] to organize my ideas. My goal is to improve my English this year. I am practicing every day.

I can speak English well, but I want to be more fluent. I am learning new words by reading stories. Let's improve our English together!

[He/She] was [courageous/kind/determined] and always helped [his/her] community. [He/She] worked hard and achieved many [achievements].

I admire [him/her] because [he/she] inspires me to [action]. [He/She] teaches us that true heroes help people.
