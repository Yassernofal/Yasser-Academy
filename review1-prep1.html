ملخص الوحدات 1-3

Unit 1: Life in a Digital World

✅ الموضوع: التكنولوجيا والحياة الرقمية.

✅ المفردات: digital, screen, ebooks, night mode, smartboard, website, laptop.

✅ القواعد: Present Simple + Adverbs of Frequency + Parts of Speech.

Unit 2: Learning to Learn

✅ الموضوع: أنماط التعلم والتعلم الجماعي.

✅ المفردات: visual, auditory, kinesthetic, mind map, organize, improve.

✅ القواعد: Present Continuous + can + Let's.

Unit 3: Role Models

✅ الموضوع: القدوة الحسنة (Dr. Samira Moussa).

✅ المفردات: hero, inspire, courageous, determined, passionate.

✅ القواعد: Past Simple (Regular + Irregular) + was/were.

مراجعة القواعد

Present Simple

✅ I/You/We/They + فعل أساسي

✅ He/She/It + فعل + s

I check my phone every morning.
أتفقد هاتفي كل صباح.
She reads ebooks at night.
تقرأ كتباً إلكترونية في الليل.

Present Continuous

✅ am/is/are + فعل + ing

I am learning English now.
أتعلم الإنجليزية الآن.
She is studying for her test.
تدرس لاختبارها.

Past Simple

✅ Regular: فعل + ed (visited, played)

✅ Irregular: شكل خاص (went, saw, ate)

✅ was/were للماضي

Dr. Samira studied physics.
درست د. سميرة الفيزياء.
She was a true hero.
كانت بطلة حقيقية.

Adverbs of Frequency

always (100%) → usually (90%) → often (70%) → sometimes (50%) → rarely (10%) → never (0%)

I always check my emails.
أتفقد بريدي دائماً.

Can + Let's

✅ can + فعل أساسي (للقدرة)

✅ Let's + فعل أساسي (للاقتراح)

I can speak English.
أستطيع التحدث بالإنجليزية.
Let's study together.
هيا ندرس معاً.

نصوص المراجعة

Tarek's Blog (Unit 1)

"Hi everyone! My name is Tarek. I always start my morning by checking my phone. I usually read three or four messages. At breakfast, I use my tablet to search for news. At school, we sometimes use the smartboard. After school, I always do my homework first. In the evening, I sometimes play online games. Before bedtime, I read ebooks on my tablet. I always use night mode."

Key words: check, tablet, smartboard, ebooks, night mode.

Learning Styles (Unit 2)

"Every person learns differently. Visual learners learn by seeing information. Auditory learners learn by hearing. Kinesthetic learners learn by moving and touching. Understanding your learning style helps you study better."

Key words: visual, auditory, kinesthetic, learn, study.

Dr. Samira Moussa (Unit 3)

"Dr. Samira Moussa was born in Egypt in 1917. She studied physics and got a PhD in atomic radiation. She taught at Cairo University. She was kind, courageous, and determined. She died in 1952, but her work still inspires people."

Key words: born, studied, taught, kind, courageous, determined, inspires.
