🌟 About Us

Mr. Yasser Nofal Academy .. A success story in English language teaching for over 35 years

Our Story

Mr. Yasser Nofal Academy was launched over 35 years ago with a clear vision: teaching English in a simple and enjoyable way suitable for all ages and levels. We started with a small class, and over the years, we have become a leading educational platform with thousands of students from all over the Arab world.

We believe that English is not just a subject, but a key to the future and a means of communication with the world. Therefore, we provide comprehensive educational content including phonics, conversations, grammar, and interactive tests.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the leading educational platform in the Arab world for teaching English, creating a confident and proficient generation.

Our Mission

To provide distinguished English education based on the latest pedagogical methods, helping learners achieve their academic and professional goals.

Our Values

Quality, creativity, respect, and excellence. We believe every student deserves the best learning opportunity.
