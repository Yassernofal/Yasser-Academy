القواعد بالتفصيل (Grammar)

Past Simple vs. Past Continuous

بنستخدم Past Simple للحدث اللي خلص. وبنستخدم Past Continuous للحدث اللي كان مستمر ولما حصل حدث تاني قطعه.

While I was walking home, I saw a shooting star.
He was watching TV when the phone rang.
1. While I ________ home, I saw a shooting star.
a) walk
b) walked
c) was walking
d) were walking
