The Power of Kindness

📖 Reading

Kindness is a language that everyone understands, even without words. A simple smile, a helping hand, or a few encouraging words can change someone's entire day. In our fast-paced world, it is easy to forget the impact of small gestures of compassion.

When we help others, we do not just make them feel better; we also improve our own well-being. Scientific studies have shown that acts of kindness release chemicals in the brain that make us feel happy and reduce stress. This is why volunteers often say they get more than they give.

There are countless ways to show kindness. You can help an elderly neighbor carry their groceries, listen to a friend who is having a bad day, or simply share a meal with someone in need. Every act, no matter how small, creates a ripple effect of positivity that spreads through communities.

Schools and workplaces are perfect places to start. By creating a culture of empathy and support, we can build environments where everyone feels valued and respected. Remember, kindness is not a sign of weakness; it is a sign of strength and emotional intelligence.

Grammar: Modal Verbs (Must - Have to)

📚 Language in Context

1. Must (يجب / لازم)

بنستخدمها عشان نتكلم عن إلزام قوي، أو حاجة بنعملها لأننا شايفينها صح أو مهمة جداً (إلزام داخلي).

I must study harder to pass the exam. (أنا مقتنع بكده)
You must see a doctor; you look very sick. (نصيحة قوية)

2. Have to / Has to (مضطر / لازم)

بنستخدمها عشان نتكلم عن إلزام خارجي، يعني قوانين، أو قواعد، أو حد تاني طالب مننا نعمل حاجة.

I have to wear a uniform at school. (قواعد المدرسة)
She has to finish her homework before dinner. (أمر من الأهل)

3. النفي (Mustn't vs Don't have to)

Mustn't: معناها (ممنوع / ميجوز) - حاجة متعملهاش خالص.

You mustn't smoke here. (ممنوع التدخين)

Don't have to: معناها (مش لازم / مش مضطر) - ممكن تعملها وممكن متعملهاش.

You don't have to come early tomorrow. (مش لازم تيجي بدري)
