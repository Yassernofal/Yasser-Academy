The Power of Kindness

📖 Reading

Kindness is a universal language that everyone understands. It is the act of showing compassion, empathy, and care towards others without expecting anything in return. A simple smile, a helping hand, or a listening ear can make a significant difference in someone's life.

In our modern world, where people are often busy with their own lives, kindness can sometimes be forgotten. However, small acts of kindness have a ripple effect. When you help someone, they are more likely to help someone else, creating a chain of positive actions that can transform communities.

Volunteering is a powerful way to practice kindness. Whether it's helping at a local charity, cleaning up a neighborhood, or tutoring younger students, volunteering allows us to contribute to society and build strong, caring communities. It also helps us develop emotional intelligence and self-awareness.

We must remember that kindness is a choice we make every day. By choosing to be kind, we not only improve the lives of others but also enrich our own lives with purpose and joy.

Grammar: Modal Verbs (Must & Have to)

📚 Language in Context

1. Must (يجب)

بنستخدم Must عشان نعبر عن إلزام قوي أو ضرورة، وغالباً بيكون رأي شخصي أو إحساس داخلي بضرورة عمل الحاجة.

We must help those in need. (It is our moral duty).
You must wear a seatbelt. (It is the law).

2. Have to (مضطر / لازم)

بنستخدم Have to عشان نعبر عن إلزام بسبب ظروف خارجية أو قواعد مفروضة علينا من حد تاني (مش رأينا الشخصي).

I have to finish my homework tonight. (Because the teacher said so).
She has to wake up early for school. (Because school starts early).

3. النفي (Negative)

Mustn't = ممنوع (إلزام بعدم العمل).

Don't have to = مش لازم (مفيش إلزام، ممكن تعمل وممكن لأ).

You mustn't smoke here. (It's forbidden).
You don't have to come if you are busy. (It's optional).
