القواعد بالتفصيل (Grammar)

Present Perfect vs. Past Simple (المضارع التام والماضي البسيط)

Present Perfect: حدث بدأ في الماضي ومازال مستمراً أو نتيجته موجودة الآن (كلمات دالة: since, for, just, already, yet, ever).
Past Simple: حدث بدأ وانتهى في الماضي (كلمات دالة: yesterday, ago, last year).
الحالات الشاذة: كلمة since يأتي بعدها نقطة زمنية (since 2010)، وكلمة for يأتي بعدها فترة زمنية (for 5 years).

I have lived in Cairo since 2015. (مازلت أعيش)
I visited my uncle yesterday. (انتهت الزيارة)
1. I ________ here since 2015, and I love this city.
a) live
b) lived
c) have lived
d) am living
2. She ________ her homework two hours ago.
a) has finished
b) have finished
c) finished
d) finishes
3. Have you ________ eaten sushi before?
a) never
b) ever
c) yet
d) since

Read the text and answer:

A healthy lifestyle is not just about eating the right food; it is a combination of physical activity, a balanced diet, and good mental health. Many teenagers today prefer fast food because it is quick and delicious. However, fast food contains high levels of fat and sugar, which can lead to obesity and heart diseases. Experts advise us to eat more fruits and vegetables, drink plenty of water, and exercise for at least 30 minutes a day. Furthermore, getting enough sleep is essential for the body to recover and for the brain to function properly.
