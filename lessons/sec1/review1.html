مراجعة القواعد

Past Simple & Past Continuous

✅ Past Simple: فعل + ed أو شكل شاذ (فعل مكتمل).

✅ Past Continuous: was/were + فعل + ing (فعل مستمر).

✅ While / When: يربطوا الفعلين.

While I was walking home, I saw a shooting star.
بينما كنت أسير للمنزل، رأيت شهاباً.

Modal Verbs (must / have to / should)

✅ must: إلزام قوي / قوانين.

✅ mustn't: منع.

✅ have to: إلزام بدون اختيار.

✅ should / shouldn't: نصيحة.

You must respect the law.
يجب أن تحترم القانون.
You should take an umbrella.
يجب أن تأخذ مظلة (نصيحة).

Conditionals (Zero, First, Second)

✅ Zero: If + Present Simple, Present Simple (حقائق).

✅ First: If + Present Simple, will + inf. (احتمال حقيقي).

✅ Second: If + Past Simple, would + inf. (حلم).

If you mix red and blue, you get purple.
إذا خلطت الأحمر والأزرق، تحصل على البنفسجي.
If it rains, we will stay at home.
إذا أمطرت، سنبقى في المنزل.
If I had money, I would travel.
إذا كان لدي مال، كنت سأسافر.

نصوص المراجعة

Egypt's Heritage (Unit 1)

"Egypt is a land with a heart that has been beating for thousands of years. Our ancestors built one of the greatest civilizations in history. They gave the world knowledge in writing, mathematics, and medicine. Today, Egypt continues to grow."

Key words: heritage, civilization, ancestors, knowledge, grow.

The Warm Bread (Unit 2)

"It was a difficult week for Sama. Then, there was a knock at the door. It was Mrs. Aya, the friendly neighbor. She brought fresh bread. Sama smiled for the first time in days. Small acts of kindness can make a big difference."

Key words: kindness, neighbor, fresh, smile, difference.

When Appearances Lie (Unit 3)

"Trust is one of the most valuable things in our lives. Some people put on a friendly face but are wolves in sheep's clothing. In the digital world, fake platforms and social media scams copy these same tactics. Verify the source before sharing."

Key words: trust, wolves in sheep's clothing, fake, scams, verify.
