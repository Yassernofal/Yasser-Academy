Part 1: Multiple Choice (Choose the correct answer)

1. While we ________ the museum, we saw a lot of ancient monuments.
a) visited
b) were visiting
c) visit
d) have visited
2. You ________ cheat in the exam. It is against the school rules.
a) don't have to
b) have to
c) mustn't
d) must
3. If I ________ you, I would check the news source before sharing it.
a) am
b) were
c) will be
d) have been
4. The ancient Egyptians ________ great pyramids thousands of years ago.
a) built
b) were building
c) build
d) have built

Part 2: Rewrite the following sentences

1. I was walking home. I saw a shooting star. (While)
Rewrite using "While":
While I was walking home, I saw a shooting star.
2. It is necessary for students to respect their teachers. (must)
Rewrite using "must":
Students must respect their teachers.
3. You are not allowed to use your mobile phone during the lesson. (mustn't)
Rewrite using "mustn't":
You mustn't use your mobile phone during the lesson.
