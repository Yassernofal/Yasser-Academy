1. The ancient Egyptians used hieroglyphs to ________ history.

a) walk

c) was walking
d) were walking

a) must
b) have to
c) don't have to
d) mustn't
4. You ________ tell anyone my secret. It's very important.
a) mustn't
b) don't have to
c) have to
d) must
5. If you ________ the news, you will avoid spreading fake information.
a) verify
b) verified
c) will verify
d) verifying
6. Kindness is not a sign of weakness; it is a sign of ________ intelligence.
a) emotional
b) emotional (صح)
c) physical
d) artificial

b) was
c) were
d) be

a) panic
b) peace
c) harmony
d) success
9. ________ you help others, you feel better about yourself.
a) If

c) Although
d) Because

b) was watching
