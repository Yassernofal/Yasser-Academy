🔒 Privacy Policy

At Mr. Yasser Nofal Academy, we prioritize your data privacy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when using Mr. Yasser Nofal Academy platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, and address.
  • Usage Data: Pages you visit and time spent on the site.
  • Payment Data: We process payments through secure services and do not store credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing and improving our educational services.
  • Sending notifications about courses and schedules.
  • Analyzing site usage to improve user experience.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following cases:

  • With service providers (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics).
  • When required by law.

4. Data Security

We apply strict security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Data encryption during transmission (SSL).
  • Storing data on secure servers.
  • Restricting data access to authorized personnel only.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our site. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to the processing of your data.

To do so, please contact us at: info@yassernofalacademy.com
